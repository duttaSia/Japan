DISCOVER KYOTO

Where Japan's history comes alive.

Kyoto is a city where centuries-old temples, traditional architecture, gardens and modern Japanese life exist side by side.

From peaceful bamboo forests to atmospheric streets filled with traditional wooden houses, Kyoto offers a slower and deeply cultural side of Japan.
