HOKKAIDO / 2026

A Snowy City of Japan

Hokkaido is Japan’s northernmost island, known for its breathtaking mountains, snowy landscapes, peaceful lakes, and beautiful countryside. From vibrant Sapporo to the lavender fields of Furano, Hokkaido offers a perfect blend of adventure, nature, culture, and relaxation.

Hokkaido Travel Spots

Explore some of Hokkaido's most memorable places.

SNOWY CAPITAL

Snowing Sapporo

Sapporo is the vibrant capital of Hokkaido, famous for its snowy winters, delicious cuisine, and lively city atmosphere.

ROMANTIC CANAL

Otaru- HOKKAIDO

Otaru is a charming coastal town in Hokkaido, famous for its romantic canal, historic warehouses, and beautiful waterfront views.

LAVENDER

LAVENDER Furano

Furano is a beautiful countryside destination in Hokkaido, famous for its colourful lavender fields, rolling hills, and breathtaking summer landscapes.
